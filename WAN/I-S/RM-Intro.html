What we are building, in the order it has to happen

First

A solid foundation

Document the $125,100 loan, pay it down, and retire the company's 21% debt. Nothing else works on top of an open exposure.

Next

Health

Family coverage paid by the business, fully deductible. From 2027 the spouse's employer plan takes over.

Next

Life protection

Today: one $165K policy. The goal: $1.5M on the owner and $1M on the spouse, so the family and the business survive the loss of either earner.

Then

Building wealth

Real salaries, a 401(k) the business funds for both spouses, Roth IRAs, and $100K a year set aside to grow the company.

Then

Estate and legacy

A second company that keeps earnings for acquisitions, funds the policies, and passes value to the family tax-efficiently.

Where we are
A growing business, a new marriage, four gaps and one loan that needs attention.
What today decides
Salary, payoff pace, the expansion reserve, insurance timing, and whether to add a second company.
Where it leads
By the end of 2027: loan gone, family insured, $80K a year saved, and a structure that can carry an acquisition.
