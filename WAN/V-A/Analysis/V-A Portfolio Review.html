Three Schwab accounts

The household's assets fall into three legal buckets that cannot be merged with each other — Vanessa's taxable money, her retirement money, and Luke's money — so three Schwab accounts is the right number, with the Centier checking account kept as the operating account that receives payroll and pays bills.

Worth a conversation

  • The margin loan is the dominant risk. At 2× leverage and a ~4.2% borrowing rate, the Robinhood account needs the holdings to return more than ~4% a year just to cover interest, and a 30% market decline — which this mix has delivered in single quarters — would force sales at the bottom. Paying the loan down to a 1.3–1.5× level would remove the margin-call risk while keeping most of the upside.
  • Same bets in both accounts. Seventeen names appear in both portfolios, and the themes are identical. The SEP IRA is not diversifying the Robinhood account; it is doubling it.
  • Retirement money at a trading tempo. The SEP IRA bought $65k and sold $28k of securities in eight months and realized a $9.5k loss while the market rose 12%. Inside the IRA there is no tax cost, but the return gap to a plain index fund this year is roughly 16 points.
  • HOOD concentration. Robinhood Markets is 17% of the IRA and, with the Robinhood-account shares, $55,956 across both — plus the brokerage relationship itself. A gain of $22.7k on cost is a good moment to trim to a position size that would not hurt.
  • Option-income ETFs. NVDY and SPYI pay out $6,233 a year on $23,691 of value, but NVDY has lost 19% of its cost; the "income" is mostly the fund returning its own capital. In an IRA there is no tax angle to justify them.
  • Idle cash is the cheapest fix available. $203,237 earning nothing while a 4.5% loan runs is a guaranteed loss of about $7,154/yr in foregone interest on top of the $9,761 of interest paid. Paying the loan with cash costs no tax and removes the margin-call risk in one move.
  • Luke's account mirrors Vanessa's. Same trades on the same day in a custodial account for a minor; the speculative tail and the option-income ETFs suit neither the purpose nor the kiddie-tax rules. Indiana529 contributions would earn a 20% state credit the UTMA does not.
  • Insurance premiums. Two life-insurance drafts ($1,400 Columbus Life, $4,000 North American) appear in one month; if these are permanent policies they are a significant ongoing cost worth reviewing alongside the investments.
  • Missing pieces. A December 2025 SEP IRA statement, the Robinhood August statement and cost-basis export, and the trust document (to confirm account titling) (Account → Statements & History → Tax documents / cost basis) would allow per-position performance for Robinhood and a proper full-year comparison across both accounts.
Method

How the numbers were built

  • Source: three PDFs in WAN\V-A. Text was extracted from the PDFs and every position table reconciles to the printed totals to the cent (SEP IRA securities $238,206.56, cost $182,343.00, unrealized $55,863.56; Robinhood securities $437,464.08; activity totals $16,592.21 / $459.27). Figures were independently re-checked against the statement text.
  • Returns are Modified Dietz with external flows at mid-period. The SEP IRA's $50,000 2026 contribution date is not on the August statement; the range quoted brackets the January and August cases. Robinhood's "market change on holdings" is closing securities minus opening securities minus purchases.
  • The SEP IRA monthly series is back-solved from the percentage changes printed on the statement's chart and therefore includes contributions; it is a value path, not a performance path.
  • Theme classification of each holding is my own. The margin-call thresholds assume a 25–30% house maintenance requirement; Robinhood sets requirements per security and may be higher for volatile names.
  • Cash recommendations use SWVXX's 3.52% 7-day yield (schwabassetmanagement.com, 14 Sep 2026) and Indiana529's 20% credit up to $1,500 (myfamilymatters.com). Monthly outflows and payroll are the 35-day Centier statement scaled to 30 days. Kiddie-tax and Indiana tax figures are approximate; confirm with the CPA.
  • A companion workbook (V-A Portfolio Analysis.xlsx) holds every transcribed figure with live formulas, including the maintenance-requirement assumption as an editable cell.
