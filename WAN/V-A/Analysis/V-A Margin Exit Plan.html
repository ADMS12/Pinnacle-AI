V-A Margin Exit Plan

The Robinhood account holds $437,464 of securities against a $216,910 margin loan — 1.98× leverage. At a 30% maintenance requirement a further 29% decline triggers a margin call, and a transfer freezes the positions for a week. The only way to make a call impossible is to move with no loan; the alternatives below show what each route costs, and the recommended sequence gets to Schwab with the risk removed.

The interest comparison matters almost as much. 3 · Recommended path

Pay it off, then move it

Alternative A is the cleanest: it removes the risk entirely, sells nothing, and stops the interest clock before the higher Schwab rate applies. If $216,910 of cash is not available, use Alternative C as the fallback — bring the loan below $122,490 with a mix of cash and low-gain sales before the transfer, then finish the payoff at Schwab. Under no circumstances should the account move with the loan at today's level. The sequence:

  1. NowTurn off the recurring buys and Gold margin auto-borrowThe weekly GOOGL and QQQI buys add about $5,750 a month to the loan. Stop them so the balance stops growing while you prepare.
  2. Week 1Export cost basis from RobinhoodAccount → Statements & History → Tax documents / cost basis. This tells you the tax cost of each candidate sale and settles the cash-versus-sell question.
  3. Week 1–2Bring the loan to zero — or, at minimum, below ~$120kBest: deposit $216,910 and pay it off (Alternative A). If that cash isn't available, deposit what you can and sell the smallest-gain or loss positions first (the sub-$3,000 tail, NVDY-style income ETFs, iShares Silver) until the loan is at or below $122,490 — that survives a 60% drop at a 30% requirement. Let all sales settle (T+1) before the next step.
  4. Week 2Tidy the fractional sharesACATS moves whole shares only. Sixteen positions carry fractions (GOOGL, MSFT, KO, CVX, WMT, SPYG, SPY, QQQI, IWMI, SCHG, PCG, MU, JD, QBTS, RKLB, GLD); Robinhood will sell the fractions, so expect a few small taxable trades. Sell them yourself if you want control of timing.
  5. Week 2Open the Schwab account as a margin accountEven if the loan will be zero, margin approval makes the transfer simpler and keeps the option. Ask Schwab about a negotiated margin rate and about reimbursing Robinhood's $100 ACATS fee — both are commonly granted at this account size.
  6. Week 3Initiate a full ACATS transfer from Schwab's sideEnter the Robinhood account number and most recent statement. Do not trade at Robinhood once submitted. Typical completion is 5–7 business days; positions and cost basis follow (basis can lag a few weeks).
  7. AfterDecide what the leverage is forWith the loan at zero, any future borrowing at Schwab is a deliberate choice at ~7–8%. If leverage is kept, cap it at 1.3× and keep a cash buffer equal to one year of interest.

Assumptions and caveats

  • Balances are from the 31 July 2026 Robinhood statement; the August statement will differ. Recalculate before acting (the companion workbook's Robinhood sheet has the formula, with the maintenance requirement as an editable cell).
  • Robinhood publishes a 4.5% rate for $100k–$1M balances; the Schwab tier rates (7.825% for $100–250k) are from a March 2026 comparison and should be confirmed with Schwab, who also set per-security maintenance requirements that can exceed 30%.
  • Robinhood's fee schedule lists a $100 outgoing ACATS fee. ACATS transfers whole shares only; fractional shares are liquidated.
  • Tax consequences of any sale depend on cost basis that Robinhood does not print on statements. This page is general information, not tax or investment advice — review the sell list with a CPA.
