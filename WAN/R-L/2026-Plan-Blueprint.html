R.L. and B LLC Case: Financial Architecture

Protecting the family, funding retirement and fixing the shareholder loan as B LLC grows from $289,584 of revenue in 2025 to $600,000 in 2026 and $750,000 in 2027. Built from the 2025 federal and California returns under 2026 rules, married filing jointly. v2 adds the spouse's $125,000 outside job from 2027, her vehicle as a business expense, a 20% employer 401(k) contribution, the existing $165K policy and the household's living costs.

⚠Core Blocker: The $125,100 Shareholder Loan

Fix before anything else

Over several years the owner took $125,100 out of B LLC as a "loan": no written note, no interest, no repayments, no collateral. It is the largest asset on the books, and without it the company is about $125K underwater. If the IRS calls it a distribution or hidden wages, the bill is roughly $28,000 to $35,000 plus penalties. Lenders and the IRS read this line first, so every scenario starts by freezing and documenting it.

Grew fast, kept little
Revenue $289,584, up 64%. Profit to the owner $69,133, down 28% and only 24% of revenue (77% in 2023). Travel, meals and vehicles $90K. Debt $171,549 at about 21% interest.

Thin margins at home
2025 income $126,792, federal tax $46, California $1,699. Married in 2026. Living costs about $17,000 a month ($204K a year); $16,000 while the spouse's vehicle is carried by B LLC. Spouse starts a $125,000 job in 2027 and keeps her $12,000 B LLC role.
3. The family's gaps
Four open risks
Undocumented loan · no health insurance · minimal life protection (one $165K policy with $15K cash value against $171K of guaranteed debt) · no retirement savings at age 31.

▭Debt Prioritization

Loan payoff first, then the 21% company debt
$125,100
Shareholder loan to document and repay
  • Steps 1 and 2: freeze all withdrawals, sign a 4% note, repay $25,000 a year from after-tax cash. Balance $75,100 at the end of 2027.
  • Steps 3 and 4: repay $62,550 in 2026 and $62,550 in 2027 through distributions the owner immediately pays back; tax-free because the income is already taxed. Loan cleared end of 2027.
  • Step 5: the C corp lends the owner the payoff at 4%: $27,366 in 2026 after the premiums, the remaining $97,734 in 2027.
  • Every repayment goes to the company's most expensive debt: Fundbox $53.6K, Headway $7K, cards $17K, vehicle loans $94K.
Sequencing imperativeSteps 3 and 4 front-load the payoff, $100K of expansion set-aside, retirement and health into 2026, leaving $91,973 (step 3) or $55,973 (step 4) against $192K of living costs. 2027 covers costs in every protected scenario once the spouse's salary arrives and the health premium drops. For 2026, start the set-aside in 2027 or spread the payoff over three years.
