This document defines Pinnacle Planning’s preliminary marketing strategy. Claude should use this information to craft marketing plans, messaging frameworks, content strategies, referral pipelines, and channel-specific campaigns targeted at business owners.

# 7. Marketing Approach

Pinnacle Planning's marketing has one job: **make the right business owner feel seen** and create a natural path to a planning session.  
Every piece of content, every referral conversation, and every networking interaction should make the ideal client think:

**“These people help people like me.”**

---

## Core Messaging

### Primary Pain Point: Taxes  
The main entry point into Pinnacle Planning is tax frustration.  
The core message:

> **"You built something. You're paying a significant tax bill every April. There are legal strategies most business owners never hear about that could shelter $100K to $300K of your income this year and turn your tax burden into your retirement plan. That conversation starts here."**

This message is simple, powerful, and universally relevant to business owners.

### Secondary Messaging  
Once the tax conversation opens the door, secondary messaging highlights the broader value of Pinnacle Planning:

- Insurance gaps  
- No coordinated plan  
- Wealth trapped in the business  
- No succession strategy  
- No trusted advisor  
- Flying blind despite making good money  

The tax pain gets them in the door.  
The full planning experience keeps them.

## Marketing Channels

### 1. Social Media (Facebook & Instagram)
- Pain-specific content targeting business owners  
- Themes: tax savings, flying blind, no real plan  
- Goal: drive traffic to a planning session booking page  
- Tone: direct, relatable, problem-first messaging  

### 2. UCLA Anderson Network
- Matthew’s primary referral and guest pipeline  
- Includes CPAs, estate attorneys, business owners, and financial professionals  
- Ideal for high-quality referral partners and clients  
- Strategy: relationship building, value-first introductions, guest speaking  

### 3. Toastmasters & Professional Networking
- In-person relationship building  
- Matthew’s elevator pitch is optimized for curiosity-driven engagement  
- Strategy: one strong statement → let them ask questions  
- Goal: build trust and create warm leads  

### 4. Referral Hub Partners
The most consistent source of qualified leads.

Key partners:
- CPAs (most natural referral source; they see tax pain every April)  
- Estate attorneys  
- Commercial bankers  
- Bookkeepers  
- Non-competing insurance agents  

Strategy:
- Long-term relationship building  
- Provide value, tools, and insights  
- Become their “go-to” advanced planning partner  

### 5. Pinnacle Planning Website & Content
- Pinnacle Planning page  
- Case studies  
- Downloadable resources (e.g., **Tax Essentials Toolkit**)  
- Purpose: credibility anchors for warm leads doing research  
- Goal: reinforce trust and accelerate booking a planning session  

### 6. Chamber of Commerce Membership (Essential)

Chamber of Commerce membership is an **essential** channel — it is where the ideal client profile (local business owners with $500K+ revenue) already congregates.

- Join and stay visibly active in local chambers (attend mixers, ribbon cuttings, and business expos)  
- Pursue leadership roles and committee positions to build authority, not just visibility  
- Offer educational value first: tax-strategy workshops, "lunch and learn" sessions, guest speaking slots  
- Sponsor select chamber events for brand presence in front of qualified owners  
- Treat fellow members as both prospects and referral hub partners (CPAs, attorneys, and bankers are members too)  
- Goal: become the chamber's recognized go-to for business-owner financial planning

## Target Markets

### Core Market

Established business owners and entrepreneurs — $500K+ business revenue, $250K–$1M+ personal income, age 40–60 — as defined in the ideal client profile.

### Emerging Market: The Hispanic Business Community

The Hispanic market is a designated **emerging market** for Pinnacle Planning — one of the fastest-growing segments of U.S. business ownership, and broadly underserved by advanced financial planning.

- Hispanic-owned businesses are among the fastest-growing business segments in the country, yet most owners have never been shown proactive tax strategy, succession planning, or coordinated advisory  
- Family, legacy, and multi-generational wealth values align naturally with Pinnacle Planning's planning domains (protection, college planning, generational wealth transfer)  
- Channel fit: Hispanic chambers of commerce (e.g., local Hispanic Chamber chapters), community business associations, and Spanish-language business events  
- Messaging: same tax-pain entry point, delivered with cultural fluency — emphasize protecting the family, the business, and the next generation  
- Develop bilingual (English/Spanish) content, lead magnets, and presentation materials over time  
- Strategy: earn trust through community presence and education first; referrals within the community compound quickly once trust is established
