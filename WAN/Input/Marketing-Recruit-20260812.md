# Proposal Prompt: Spanish-Speaking Sales & Marketing Partners for Pinnacle Planning

## Context
Pinnacle Planning plans to hire or partner with **two Spanish-speaking salespeople** to support both **marketing** and **sales** functions. These individuals will help expand outreach to Spanish-speaking business owners and improve lead generation, appointment setting, and eventual sales conversion.

Use the outline below to craft a complete proposal, including structure, justification, responsibilities, compensation, and integration into Pinnacle Planning’s existing marketing and sales workflow.

---

## Goals

### Primary Goal
- **Marketing: Lead Generation**
  - Expand outreach to Spanish-speaking business owners
  - Increase booked planning sessions
  - Build awareness of Pinnacle Planning’s tax-saving and financial planning solutions

### Secondary Goal
- **Sales: Recruitment**
  - Identify qualified prospects
  - Support sales pipeline development
  - Assist in onboarding clients into Pinnacle Planning’s 4-meeting sales cycle

---

## Target Avatar (Ideal Client Profile)
- Spanish-speaking business owners
- “Eddie” profile:
  - Business tax savings needs
  - Personal and business protection
  - Retirement planning
  - Family trust setup
  - Generational wealth transfer

---

## Methods (Marketing & Outreach Activities)
- **Cold Calls**
  - Spanish-language outreach to local business owners
  - Scripted tax-savings messaging
- **Local Business Site Visits**
  - In-person introductions
  - Distribution of Pinnacle Planning marketing materials
  - Appointment setting for planning sessions

---

## Cost / Compensation Structure

  - Mileage
  - Meals

  - $100 per qualified appointment
- **Marketing Fees:**  
  - 10% marketing compensation
