How the numbers are computed

  • Source: Schwab Order Status export . Only option orders marked Filled or Closed partial fill are used for P&L. Stock sells (AAPL, COST, BRK/B and others) are listed in the log but are not matched into trades because their purchases are outside this export.
  • Matching: each Buy to open lot is paired with later Sell to close fills on the same contract, first in first out. A buy of 5 that is sold 3 then 2 becomes two trades. P&L = (exit − entry) × 100 × contracts, before commissions and fees.
  • Expired: a lot never sold before its expiration date is counted as closed at $0.00 at expiry (exit type "Expired"). That is a full loss of premium, which is the realistic outcome for 0DTE SPY and SPX options held through the close.
  • Unmatched sells: These closed positions opened before the export window and are excluded.
  • "Add down / add up": a Buy to open placed while already holding the same contract, at a lower (down) or higher (up) price than the existing lots. "First" means a fresh position.
  • Hold time: minutes from the lot's buy fill to the sell fill (or expiry).
