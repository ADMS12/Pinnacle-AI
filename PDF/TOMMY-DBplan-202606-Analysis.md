## 6. Findings and recommendations

### 6.1 New contributions are defaulting to money market
Allocation on file is **100% EQ/Money Market**. Any plan contribution deposited into this contract sits in cash until someone manually assigns it to segments. If a 2026 plan-year contribution is coming, an allocation instruction should be set before the deposit or immediately after.

### 6.2 The plan is 100% equity-linked with no fixed or guaranteed sleeve
A defined benefit plan carries an actuarial assumed rate of return and a minimum funding obligation. The buffers reduce, but do not eliminate, drawdown risk: 51% of the plan has only a 10% buffer. If the plan's actuarial assumption is in the 5–6% range, the current structure carries more downside than needed to meet it. Worth confirming with the plan's TPA/actuary what the assumed return and current funded status are.

### 6.3 Segment fragmentation
129 segments, 23 of them under $3,000 and the smallest at $1,527.66. Each 6-year segment is a separate lock with its own maturity date. This makes the plan hard to monitor and rebalance. As 1-year segments mature over the next 12 months, consolidate into fewer, larger positions.

### 6.4 Re-evaluate every 6-year segment issued in 2024–2025 at low caps
The S&P Annual Lock segments at 7.0% and 5.5% caps and several Enhanced Upside segments issued late in 2024–2025 were bought at the low end of Equitable's cap cycle. They cannot be exited early without taking interim value. Nothing to do now, but they should not be renewed on the same terms at maturity.

### 6.5 Small-cap and international overweight
Russell 2000 at 24% and EAFE+EM at 22% is a deliberate value/diversification tilt. The Russell Annual Lock segments have been the plan's worst performers. Whether to keep this tilt is a judgment call, but it should be a conscious one at each maturity rather than the default rollover.

### 6.6 Caps are currently favorable for 1-year rollovers
The July 2026 rollovers picked up caps of 10.6% (EAFE) and 17.5% (NASDAQ 100), up from 8.7% and 12.0%. Higher caps generally reflect higher market volatility or higher interest rates. If this holds through the fall 2026 maturities ($135K), same-type rollovers will be on better terms than a year ago.

### 6.7 Information not in this statement (needed for a full evaluation)
- Total contributions to date (to compute the contract's actual internal rate of return since February 2021)
- The plan's actuarial assumed rate and funded status
- Whether other assets exist in the DB plan outside this contract
- Fee schedule and any surrender charge schedule still in force (contract is 5.4 years old)
- Prior statement (for period-over-period change in account value)
