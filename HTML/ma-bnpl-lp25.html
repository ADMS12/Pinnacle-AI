Risk & Protections

Where the risk actually sits, and what protects the LP.

The LP’s capital is at risk — this is an equity-style limited partnership, not a loan. However, the structure includes several protections that materially reduce downside exposure relative to a pure equity investment.

Protection 1

Milestone-tied tranches

After the $40K seed, the remaining $80K is released in $8K monthly tranches. If Pinnacle misses the beta or MA launch milestones by more than 30 days, LP may halt further tranches — capping downside at the funded portion.

Protection 2

Preferred return

100% of the LP’s 25% share is classified as return-of-capital until the full $120,000 is recouped. Co-Card’s 75% slice and Pinnacle’s share continue to flow during this phase; only the LP receives priority allocation.

Protection 3

Observer + audit rights

LP receives quarterly financial reporting, monthly distribution statements, and audit rights on Co-Card’s revenue ledgers. An observer seat at partnership meetings keeps the LP informed without operational burden.

Key Risk 1

Volume ramp risk

The model assumes 20 MA loans in August scaling to 150/month by May 2027. If actual volume tracks 50% of plan, LP cumulative receipts would be ~$240K — still a 2.0× on capital, but capital recovery slips into Q1 2027.

Key Risk 2

Technology delivery

If Pinnacle AI fails to deliver a working MA platform by August 2026, no revenue flows. The milestone-tranche structure mitigates this: LP is exposed only to the $40K seed plus any tranches already released before the slip.

Key Risk 3

Co-Card execution

Co-Card’s existing merchant book and sales team must convert. Co-Card’s own 75% economic interest is fully aligned with maximizing volume — their incentive to perform is structurally identical to the LP’s.

Aligned incentives. The LP’s 25% is carved from Co-Card’s slice — not Pinnacle’s — precisely because Co-Card is the party that controls revenue. Every Co-Card dollar earned is 25 cents to the LP and 75 cents to Co-Card. There is no scenario where Co-Card prospers and the LP does not.
