Partnership Overview

Who does what, and how value flows.

Co-Card is a credit card processor with an established book of merchants. Pinnacle AI is the technology provider with experience building MA and BNPL systems. Co-Card seeds development and leases the platform; Pinnacle AI builds, hosts, and continually improves it. Both parties earn from every reseller and every transaction.

Pinnacle AI

Builds the platform

Develops, hosts, and maintains the MA and BNPL systems. Provides the technology backbone, integrations, and ongoing engineering.

Co-Card

Brings the market

Leverages existing merchant relationships to sell resellers, sublease the platform, and run sales, marketing, and underwriting teams.

Together

Share the upside

Recurring license fees + transaction fees split between both parties. Once 10 MA resellers are recruited, the lease pays for itself.

System Licensing (Lease)

A low-barrier, milestone-tied 12-month commitment.

Co-Card funds $40,000 at project start to kick off development, then leases the system for $8,000/month across the remainder of the 12-month term — a $120,000 total commitment. As Co-Card recruits resellers, sublease revenue offsets the lease.

Why this works

Aligned incentives. Predictable build. Compounding revenue.

Low capital risk

$40K seed plus milestone-tied $8K lease payments. Co-Card sees a working beta before the second tranche and a live MA launch before month 3.

Recurring revenue

Every MA sub-licensee adds $5K/mo. Every BNPL vendor adds $99/mo. Recurring SaaS layered on top of transaction-based fees.

Built-in payback

10 MA resellers fully offset Co-Card’s monthly lease. Anything beyond that — plus the $250/loan transaction fees — is upside for both sides.
