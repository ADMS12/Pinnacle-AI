Executive Summary

Not an exchange. Not a custodian. A smarter front door.

PredictAI sits between the customer and the regulated market plumbing. The heavy regulatory machinery — custody, clearing, matching, listing — stays with the exchange and the FCM. PredictAI focuses on what it can do best: acquiring customers and making them smarter traders with AI.

The company will NOT

Out of scope by design

  • Operate an exchange
  • Hold customer funds
  • Perform clearing
  • Match orders
  • List contracts
PredictAI WILL

The value it delivers

  • Acquire customers
  • Provide AI-powered market intelligence
  • Offer a superior trading interface
  • Route customer orders through an FCM
  • Earn transaction-based and subscription revenue (subject to partner agreements)
Vision

The leading AI-powered IB

Become the leading AI-powered introducing broker for prediction markets in North America.

Mission

Intelligence + access

Deliver institutional-grade AI market intelligence while providing seamless access to regulated prediction markets.

Company Structure

Two brands: a technology company and a regulated brokerage.

Rather than a single “PredictAI Brokerage” entity, the recommended structure separates the technology platform from the regulated Introducing Broker business — giving the company flexibility to license its AI platform beyond its own IB operation and making it more attractive to investors.

Operation Company

PredictAI Technologies, Inc.

The legal entity that owns the software, intellectual property, and AI platform. In the future it can license its AI platform, analytics, and software to other brokerages, FCMs, or financial institutions — value beyond the brokerage business.

Operating Division

PredictAI Brokerage

The regulated Introducing Broker business: customer acquisition, the trading interface, and order routing to partner FCMs under the agreed regulatory framework.

Legal Entity

Regulatory Strategy

A Guaranteed IB — the FCM carries the regulatory weight.

PredictAI will operate as a Guaranteed Introducing Broker under an FCM, keeping the compliance-heavy functions with the party built to handle them.

Strategic Customer Acquisition Advantage

An existing industry network of 58,000 nail salons.

PredictAI has access to an established business network within the beauty industry — approximately 58,000 U.S. nail salons with hundreds of thousands of salon owners, technicians, employees, and customers, plus thousands of local businesses (car repair shops, restaurants, dentists, tea shops). This network is a distribution channel that can be leveraged through partnerships, referrals, and targeted marketing.
