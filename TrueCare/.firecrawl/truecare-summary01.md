**Executive Opportunity Summary**

True Care System is a cloud-based enterprise SaaS platform developed for Home Care, I/DD, Behavioral Health, and Community-Based Service providers.

The platform integrates patient management, workforce management, scheduling, EVV, billing, payroll, compliance, reporting, mobile applications, and enterprise administration into one unified healthcare ecosystem.

Developed from real-world healthcare operations, True Care System has evolved into a production-ready platform featuring HIPAA-oriented security, Sandata EVV Production integration, multi-tenant cloud architecture, and AI-ready technology.

After more than three years of product development, real-world operational validation, and continuous platform enhancement, True Care System has evolved into a production-ready healthcare platform and is now entering its commercialization phase, focused on strategic partnerships and multi-state expansion.

The Company has successfully completed the most resource-intensive phase of product development and has transitioned from concept validation into commercial readiness.

Key achievements include:

- Production-ready multi-tenant SaaS platform

- Support for complete healthcare operational workflows

These accomplishments significantly reduce technology execution risk and provide a strong foundation for commercialization, customer acquisition, strategic partnerships, and future expansion across Pennsylvania and other Sandata-supported states.
