At a glance

AI Scheduling is live on the test build

The AI Scheduling and Selena assistant features proposed in the MCP plan have been implemented in True Care System and deployed to the test environment. This page walks through what was built, on both the admin web app and the DSP mobile app, using screenshots from the build so the design can be reviewed before it goes to production.

Admin web app

  • AI Assist, per Individual. From the Schedule page, the AI ranks the best DSP for every shift in an Individual's master schedule, explains why, and lets the scheduler override before approving.
  • AI Auto-Schedule, all Individuals (Advanced). Fills or re-plans every shift across the provider for a date range, shows projected payroll cost, and can be undone as one batch for 24 hours.
  • AI Scheduling Settings. Each provider sets its own scoring weights and thresholds to match its staffing strategy.
  • Selena chat for schedulers. Ask for replacements when a DSP is out, then let Selena apply the reassignment or do it by hand.
  • Printable Report Summary. Every applied batch can be printed as a per-Individual list of new, assigned, replaced and failed shifts.

DSP mobile app

  • Selena AI Assistant reachable from the home screen icon at top right.
  • Answers questions about the DSP's own shifts today, this week, next shift, and open backup shifts.
  • Strict scope. Selena refuses to look up any other employee's schedule and cannot claim shifts on the DSP's behalf.
Design principle carried over from the proposal

AI proposes, a human approves. Every AI suggestion lands in a review dialog with an override dropdown, and nothing is written to the schedule until a supervisor clicks Approve and confirms their password.

How it works

The same loop applies whether the scheduler works one Individual at a time or runs the whole provider. The AI only ever produces a proposal. The system applies it after approval and keeps a snapshot so it can be reversed.

1 · Master schedule
Scheduler sets service and time for each weekday. DSP left empty.
2 · AI Scheduling
Click the button, choose Per Individual or All Individuals.
3 · Candidate search
Every qualified DSP scored on availability, conflicts, overtime, distance, cost, preference.
4 · Proposal
Top pick per shift with the reasons shown. Shifts with no fit are flagged, never guessed.
5 · Review & approve
Override any pick, choose 1 to 4 weeks, confirm password, Approve.
6 · Apply & sync
Snapshot taken, conflicts re-checked, weekly schedule generated, DSPs see it on mobile.

03

AI Assist, per Individual

Admin web appImplemented

The everyday path for a scheduler: pick one Individual, define the master schedule, and let the AI fill in the DSPs shift by shift.

  1. Open the Schedule menu and select an Individual

    The Schedule page shows the monthly calendar overview plus the Individual's master and weekly schedules. After an AI run, a confirmation banner reports what was applied.

  2. Set the master schedule with no DSP, then click AI Scheduling

    Each weekday card carries the service code and time window. The DSP is intentionally left blank. The yellow AI Scheduling button sits in the weekly schedule toolbar next to Weekly detail, Summary & conflicts, and Payroll & ISP.

  3. Review the suggested DSP for every shift

    The proposal dialog lists each shift with the AI's top pick preselected in a dropdown, its score, and the reasons behind it. The scheduler can pick any other candidate from the dropdown. If the shift already has a DSP, it is shown as "Current DSP" so the suggestion can be compared against it. When no DSP fits, the shift is flagged for manual handling instead of being filled.

  4. Choose how many weeks to generate and approve

    "Generate for" accepts 1 to 4 weeks. Approve applies the DSP names to the master schedule, generates the weekly shifts for the chosen span, and pushes them to the DSPs' mobile apps. Before it runs, the user is asked to confirm their password (see Guardrails).

Why this matters

A provider that is short-staffed can push Availability and Distance up so more shifts get filled. A provider watching payroll can raise Cost and Overtime. The same engine serves both without code changes, and the settings are protected by the same password confirmation as the scheduling actions.

05

AI Auto-Schedule, all Individuals (Advanced)

Admin web appImplementedSupervisor only

For providers with hundreds of Individuals, a supervisor can have the AI draft DSP assignments for every shift in a date range in seconds. Because one approval can rewrite the whole provider's schedule, this mode carries extra safeguards: a projected-cost preview, an explicit confirmation, a per-shift snapshot, and a 24-hour undo.

Choosing the mode

The Advanced workflow

  1. Set the date range and options

    Start and end date are required. The Individual dropdown is optional; leaving it on "All Individuals" runs the whole provider. The checkbox "Allow AI to also suggest replacing DSPs already manually assigned" is off by default, so the AI only fills shifts that have no DSP yet unless the supervisor deliberately opts in.

  2. Review the batch proposal and projected cost

    The results header summarizes the run: shifts processed, shifts with no suitable DSP, and the projected payroll cost with the change versus last week. Shifts the AI could not fill are called out and left unassigned. Each row shows the Individual, date, time, service, the AI pick with its reasoning, and, when a DSP is being swapped, a "Replacing" label with the previous name. Rows where the AI keeps the existing DSP read "current".

  3. Confirm the change

    Approve triggers a final confirmation that states the number of shifts affected and the undo limit. On OK the system removes the old DSPs where applicable, re-runs the conflict check across all Individuals and DSPs, and writes the new assignments.

  4. Undo within 24 hours if anything is wrong

    Before any shift is changed, its status and DSP are captured in a snapshot tied to a batch ID. The Advanced dialog then shows a banner with the batch size and the exact time the undo window closes, plus an Undo last batch button. The window lasts 24 hours from the last action and stays visible to supervisors who reopen the dialog. Undo restores the previous DSP on every shift in the batch.

Why the extra care

A single Advanced approval can change the schedule for every Individual in a provider across many days. A mistake here is expensive and hard to unwind by hand, which is why this mode adds the snapshot, the batch undo, the cost preview, the opt-in for replacing existing DSPs, and the password confirmation on top of the normal review step.

06

Selena on the web: replacements by conversation

Admin web appImplemented

The most common scheduling emergency is a DSP who suddenly cannot work. Selena, the in-app assistant, handles it in plain language: name the DSP and the dates, get ranked replacements per shift, then choose whether to apply the change yourself or let Selena do it and report back.

Scenario A: a DSP is out for three days

Scheduler
I need to find a replacement for Alexander Taylor from September 10 to September 12, 2026.
Selena
Shift: September 11, 2026, 07:00 to 11:00 AM, Individual Michael Jackson, In-Home & Community Support Services.

1. Joseph Thompson (match 0.9): projected 4 hours this week, 0.3 miles from the client, $18.00/hr
2. Charlotte Lopez (match 0.8): projected 11 hours this week, 0.2 miles from the client, $40.00/hr

Would you like to update this shift yourself, or would you like me to update it automatically?
Scheduler
I'll update it myself, thanks.
Selena
Thank you. Please remember to make the changes directly in the Weekly Schedule.

Scenario B: replace a DSP for a full week, applied automatically

Selena confirms the name when the spelling is off ("Did you mean Raymond Wu?"), lists replacement candidates for each of the DSP's shifts in the range, and on confirmation reassigns them all and returns a summary. The weekly schedule reflects the change immediately.

Scenario C: partial reassignment, and unclaimed backup shifts

Selena also handles narrower instructions. Asked to move only three specific dates from Benjamin Hernandez back to Raymond Wu, it first shows the DSP's upcoming shifts, then notices an unclaimed backup shift in the same range and asks whether to include it. The answer to the question "how do I pull a DSP out for emergency time off and have the AI re-plan with the remaining staff" is this flow: Selena scans for shifts in BACKUP OPEN status first and offers to fill them at the same time.

Two ways to finish

Selena always offers the choice: apply the reassignment automatically and report the details, or leave it to the scheduler to edit the weekly schedule by hand. For a provider with hundreds of Individuals, the automatic path re-plans a DSP's whole week in seconds.

Report Summary after an AI run

Admin web appImplemented

Once a batch has been applied, the supervisor needs a record of exactly what changed, for the office file, for payroll, and for the DSPs who are affected. The Advanced dialog now ends with a status line and a Print Report Summary button that produces a printable, per-Individual summary of the whole batch.

What the report records

StatusMeaningExample from the sample
Applied (New shift)The AI created a shift that did not exist before and assigned a DSP.Bennett Chloe, Sun Sep 13, Licensed Practical Nurse, Avery Ramirez
Applied (Assign)An existing open shift with no DSP was filled.Brown Benja, Sat Sep 12, Companion Services, Daniel Jackson
Applied (Replace)A shift that already had a DSP was reassigned. Only happens when the replace option was ticked.Brooks Xavier, Sun Sep 13, Companion Services, Tu Chau
FailedThe shift was left unassigned, with the reason printed inline, so the scheduler can act on it by hand.Bruce Marcus, Mon Sep 14, "No DSP was selected for this shift."
Why it matters

The report is the paper trail for the batch. It ties the undo window's batch to a human-readable list of every shift touched, separates new shifts from replacements, and puts the failures in the same document so nothing is lost when the dialog is closed. Printed or saved as PDF, it can go to the office file, to payroll, or to the DSPs affected.

Selena for DSPs on the mobile app

Mobile appImplemented

DSPs get the same assistant, scoped to themselves. The Selena icon at the top right of the home screen opens a chat that answers questions about the DSP's own shifts and hours, and nothing else.

On the Mobile app, I can only help you with your own schedule, shifts, and hours. I do not have access to look up other employees' schedules. Please check the Web portal or contact the Office for that information.

How to test it yourself

The feature is on the test environment at test-app.truecaresystem.com. This is the walkthrough provided with the build.

  1. Open Schedule and pick an Individual

    From the top menu choose Schedule, then select one Individual.

  2. Set the master schedule

    Add the service and time for each weekday you want covered. Leave the DSP empty.

  3. Click AI Scheduling and choose AI Assist, per Individual

    The AI searches every DSP who satisfies availability, no conflicts, qualification, the 40-hour week and distance, and puts the best one at the top of each shift.

  4. Review, override if you like, set Generate for 1 to 4 weeks

    Swap any pick from the dropdown. Shifts with no suitable DSP are flagged for you.

  5. Approve and confirm your password

    The DSP names appear on the master schedule, the weekly schedules are generated for the span you chose, and the DSPs receive the shifts in the mobile app.

  6. Try Selena

    Open the Selena panel and ask, for example, "I need to find a replacement for [DSP] from [date] to [date]." Then try the Advanced mode with a short date range and use Undo last batch.
